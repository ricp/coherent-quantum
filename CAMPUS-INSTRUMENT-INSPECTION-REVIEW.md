## Limits retained deliberately

Factories remain authored equipment housings rather than a physically simulated manufacturing interior. The cutaway can leave contextual upper-floor furniture visible without its architectural slab. These are stylized campus representations. Connections are representative schematics; numbered equipment uses whole-laboratory diagnostics. Authored workflow bands do not measure actual per-stage execution, and the browser does not perform the large workload its scenario describes. Human enjoyment, blind usability, physical mobile-device input/frame rate, and a new full campaign run were not established by this bounded review. No raw save fixture is part of the deliverable.
