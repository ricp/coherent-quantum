# Coherent: an evolving quantum laboratory

The user playtested and rejected the compact 3D instrument as boring and visually static. They explicitly requested a huge, stunning, enjoyable laboratory. The compact version is saved in local commit 960dd19 and 3D-CHECKPOINT.md. This direction supersedes its isolated-board compositions and the old restriction against depicting a room.

## Accepted implemented direction

The parent and independent AI 3D specialist agree on a substantial cutaway facility: a tall copper cryostat, elevated coax routes, research desks, control cabinets, processor infrastructure, and later classical/logical/construction wings. The facility must occupy the full instrument width, keep a strong architectural silhouette, and feel active and inhabited. A larger static floor is insufficient.

The separate index-3d.html shares the unchanged game engine, campaign, paper archive and saves. The original instrument and next-action controls move to a full-width theater in this entry only. A sticky native action bar makes the player's next experiment accessible above the facility. The 2D entry remains unchanged. Successful upgrades offer a fixed inspection cue without moving routine purchase controls; returning reveals the actual changed apparatus. Follow mode brings new experiments into view from Overview, while manual inspection remains under player control.

## Activity and motion

Use actual metrics for ongoing calibration, delivered customer work, classical effort/design output and construction. When a stream has zero flow, its corresponding activity stops. Purposeful motion should visibly connect decisions to the apparatus. Production is illustrative; optical routing cues are not real microwave timing, unknown-state readout or transport of stored quantum information.

Use one WebGL renderer and a bounded render driver. Existing app draw calls update the renderer's input; avoid competing GPU render loops. Instancing, pooled meshes, finite equipment cohorts and capped pixel ratio bound cost. Paused, completed, reduced-motion, hidden, offscreen, non-laboratory and chapter-modal views avoid continuous scene work. A reveal interrupted by scrolling resumes when the apparatus is inspected. User-triggered inspection remains available.

## Independent AI reviewer acceptance criteria

1. The opening resembles entering a substantial laboratory, with dense purposeful composition and a tall cryostat centerpiece.
2. Station cameras reveal materially different viewpoints and equipment.
3. Legal purchases visibly grow the facility within chapters, including staff, racks, hardware, pulse, decoder and workshop changes.
4. Installed versus supported capacity and fabrication versus integration remain distinguishable.
5. Ongoing work uses truthful stream activity and preserves the game's limiting resources.
6. A purchase or research event has a memorable short assembly/reveal cue with stable controls.
7. Actual 320px/375px and desktop views preserve readable labels, controls, next action and page scrolling.
8. Pause, hidden views, reduced motion and zero-flow conditions stop the appropriate activity.
9. Maximum supported footprint/resource probes remain bounded, repeated changes stabilize, and 2D fallback plus export/import work.
10. A recording shows an actual legal purchase/activity sequence. A scene tour alone is insufficient.

Actual enjoyment, physical-device performance and first-human-play pacing require user/device feedback. Source review, scene captures and simulated legal actions cannot establish those claims.
