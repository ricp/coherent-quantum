# Independent quantum review and consensus

The first research phase is complete. The lead analysis and a separate AI agent assigned to independently review quantum computing reached consensus on the proposed campaign, scientific rules, source critique, paper selection, and appropriate scientific visual metaphors on 4 October 2026.

This is an independent **AI scientific design review**. No credentialed human specialist was contacted, and Keir was not contacted. It is not experimental replication, hardware validation, or a claim that the future game's economy and visual implementation have already been tested.

## What was reviewed

- The original downloaded HTML, especially its Regev project and quantum treasury event.
- [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md), including the original game's quantum and consciousness storytelling limits.
- [QUANTUM-DESIGN.md](QUANTUM-DESIGN.md), including architecture, progression, measurement, noise, patch footprints, workload budgets, factories, decoding, validation, advantage claims, endings, and interface metaphors.
- [PAPERS.md](PAPERS.md), its 36 primary quantum sources, and the proposed 30-discovery mapping.

The reviewer independently browsed primary papers, abstracts, metadata, and selected full text. The lead also checked central papers. Some publisher destinations restricted automated access; alternate arXiv or author-hosted sources and DOI metadata supported the bibliography. This review does not imply every article was read in full or every link's publisher content was accessible.

## Initial agreement

Both reviewers preferred a single superconducting hardware campaign with surface-code error correction. That scope gives calibration, cooling, control, two-dimensional layouts, classical decoding, and factories a consistent meaning. An all-architecture campaign would introduce scientific and economic differences without helping the first game's core loop.

Both agreed that the game should reward qualified experiments and named workloads. Raw qubit count, entanglement, or Hilbert-space size must not directly become a universal quantum-power or revenue multiplier. Scientific history and the order of campaign unlocks must remain distinct.

For the UI, both favored pulse traces, measured histograms, chip connectivity, syndrome detection events, logical allocation, and visible resource bottlenecks. They rejected visual metaphors that imply unknown quantum states are directly readable, quantum information can be freely copied, or entanglement permits instantaneous communication.

## First draft review

The first draft received **REQUEST CHANGES** on three narrow but material scientific distinctions. Its overall concept and original-game quantum critique were accepted.

| Issue | Why it mattered | Revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A no-fault product lacked explicit independence assumptions | Markovian noise does not by itself justify multiplying marginal event probabilities; the exponential approximation also needs small errors | The design now restricts the product to independent stochastic faults and states the small-error condition separately |
| A fitted error estimate was written as an upper-bound inequality | A union-bound expression using point estimates is a conditional model budget, not a rigorous experimentally certified bound | The design defines `B_model`, separates memory-cycle and per-gate terms, avoids double charging, and distinguishes fitted estimates from validated upper bounds |
| A feasible future workload could be read as a genuinely computed and verified result | A browser economy does not execute a classically inaccessible fault-tolerant quantum computation | The design requires a validation method for actual tutorials and explicitly labels large future tasks as modeled scenario completions; modeled speedup is separate from real-world measurement |

The reviewer also recommended saying expansion **can introduce** crosstalk or correlated errors, rather than treating them as inevitable. That wording was adopted.

The lead accepted these corrections because they made the scientific meaning more precise without complicating the game. The union-bound revision also explicitly states that the mathematical bound does not require independent or disjoint events. Avoiding double counting refers to accounting categories, not an independence requirement for the union bound.

## Second draft review

The reviewer reread the revised design, source critique, and bibliography and returned **ACCEPT**. All three required changes and the optional wording correction were satisfied. The reviewer additionally checked selected arXiv DOI metadata and primary author records and found no necessary bibliographic corrections.

The accepted scientific rules are:

1. Architecture and educational-model assumptions are explicit.
2. Shots and statistical confidence are separate from systematic bias and fidelity.
3. The circuit-noise proxy declares its assumptions and is not algorithm-success certification.
4. Standalone patch accounting stays distinct from approximate tile layouts and complete machine requirements.
5. Below-threshold memory qualification stays distinct from a universal logical gate stack.
6. Workload budgets include compatible memory, gate, factory, preparation, and other terms without double charging.
7. Decoder streaming throughput stays distinct from feedback-critical latency.
8. Runtime includes applicable sequential work, repetitions, and hardware overhead; overlapping streams justify a maximum only where the schedule allows it.
9. Actual output validation stays distinct from resource feasibility and future scenario completion.
10. Advantage comparisons identify the same task, accuracy, success probability, and a named dated baseline.
11. Real research, dated resource estimates, and fictional future scenarios carry different evidence labels.
12. The gift's visual ambition uses scientifically appropriate metaphors and remains a proposed direction until implemented and inspected.

## What consensus does and does not finish

There are no remaining scientific objections to this **proposal** from the independent reviewer. The complete original-game analysis, project inventory, source checks, legal winning simulation, bibliography, and design brief are saved locally.

Implementation still needs hardware scenario tables, workload instances, gate/decoder/factory resource data, prices, pacing, save behavior, complete strategy testing, final art, and desktop/mobile visual QA. Those are explicitly unbuilt parts of the new game. Consensus is not a substitute for those checks, and the surprise gift has not been published or sent.

The reviewer independently checked the depicted rotated-patch construction at distances 3, 5, 7, and 9: `d²` data qubits, `d²−1` ancillas, totals 17/49/97/161, commuting opposite checks, and independent check counts. The graphics use selected coherence scenarios, actual classically simulated sample groups, an exact classical two-spin curve/reference, conditional allocation budgets, and one-execution schedule lanes followed by the repeated total.

This consensus accepts the **declared educational model, citation identities and mappings, small classical tutorial/certificates, scientific interface wording, and source-level graphics geometry**. It does not establish experimental hardware reliability, universal resource estimates, reproduction of every cited study, numerical quantum performance prediction, or demonstrated advantage. Browser visual inspection, accessibility, artistic judgment, and complete gameplay verification remain distinct evidence; scientific acceptance alone does not prove them.
