The short campaign is preserved at local commit `ac4e485`. The user's first play took 15:33, versus 1h33 for Singular Value. That is evidence that the original Coherent economy failed the intended depth and pacing, despite its functional and scientific checks.

## What is being adapted

| Original mechanism | Quantum laboratory adaptation | Decision preserved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Researchers and notebooks share credibility | Research assignments and notebook storage share earned game trust | Production competes with the capacity needed for the next discovery |
| Insight is bounded; ideas accrue four times faster while full | Abstract research effort is bounded; consolidated notes redirect classical design work | Spending research sacrifices design throughput; expanding storage delays consolidation |
| Training, agents, and customers share compute | Experiments, classical bench automation, and known-preparation customer batches share apparatus/controller duty | Revenue and internal progress compete for delivered capacity |
| Price changes demand; only fulfilled requests pay | Price-elastic bounded demand pays only for currently qualified delivered batches | Higher prices can leave capacity unused; automation can crowd out customers |

| Openness and rollout forks | Explicit economic dissemination and commissioning choices | Future demand, margin, trust, construction rate, and maintenance differ |

Assignments can be released. Reducing storage below already banked effort is rejected with an explanation; it never silently discards that effort. This deliberately repairs the original's irreversible staffing trap while retaining its strategic pressure.

## Scientific boundary

Trust, effort, designs, prices, customer demand, maintenance coefficients, construction rates, and laboratory seconds are game management assumptions. Designs are classical engineering plans; they are not quantum states or newly demonstrated scientific facts. The 36 primary sources and paper notes remain accessible.

Customer work is a selected batch of 1,024 known Bell-state preparations measured in the computational basis. A rack preset supplies a declared number of disjoint control/readout pair lanes. Contract cutoffs are game eligibility rules, not experimentally certified accuracy. Classical automation uses controller batch-equivalent capacity and does not acquire a quantum speedup. It may work while quantum customer quality is unqualified.

Post-calibration duty is shared between science and services. Automation reserves service capacity before customers. Protected-memory, logical-gate, factory, and full fault-tolerant schedules reserve their apparatus: customer batches, bench automation, and commissioning construction pause. Their complete modeled physical schedule is not interrupted by customer state preparation or newly allocated footprint. Pacing laboratory seconds remain distinct from modeled physical microseconds.

Construction progress adds only completed integer qubits to installed or supported totals, with a selected ceiling. Active physical capacity remains the minimum. The existing separate noise, patch, decoding, gate, factory, repetition, and workload budgets still determine readiness.

## Acceptance and pacing

The provisional target is about 90 minutes for an active first human play. Knowledgeable legal players may be faster. Neither simulated duration nor larger costs prove that human target.

The independent AI game-design reviewer has accepted the implemented revision and its stated evidence scope. The reviewed criteria were:

1. At least two adaptive policies finish with different investment, assignment, and hardware histories.
2. Greedy staffing or frozen allocation creates a visible disadvantage that can be recovered.
3. Funding, notebook capacity, designs, apparatus throughput, and reliability each constrain progress somewhere.
4. Spending effort versus retaining a full bank changes design production in successful runs.
5. Pricing and maintenance chores retire through earned automation.
6. A poor allocation or failed schedule can recover without resetting.
7. The original 15-minute strategy cannot bypass the new economy.
8. Full-bank waits are reported explicitly, including long intervals without useful actions.

The independent AI quantum reviewer separately accepted the implemented scientific accounting and claims under the boundaries above. An independent AI source reviewer accepted the engine and interface changes. `REVIEW.md` records the actual conclusions and their limits. No human specialist has been contacted.

## Measured revision

The connected engine, content, and interface are implemented. Initial tuning was rejected when designs dominated 98% of progress; the accepted revision adds earned midgame efficiencies and reversible analysis duty. Twenty legal matrix runs across four policies and five seeds finish in 69–82 minutes, with 320 saved-state checks. Eighteen matched comparisons demonstrate investment, dissemination, pricing, synthesis, and allocation consequences. The fastest tested comparison takes 62:10. These are main-ending routes with three optional discoveries remaining, comparable to the user's original 27/30 finish.

Openness wins in the matched two-station plan; proprietary service wins with four stations. A larger automation target can take longer than a smaller investment, and the actual installed count is reported rather than treating the target as a purchase. A frozen full-analysis plan stalls with four owned stations and no funding; the same saved laboratory finishes after reducing analysis duty, without resetting, selling stations, injecting resources, or removing discoveries.

Twenty-three actual browser checks and desktop/mobile captures verify the changed interface, native controls, version separation, calibration recovery, and clear current constraints. `DEPTH-VERIFICATION.md` maps implementation criteria to the engine, browser, and review evidence.

The longest reference gap between recorded investments, assignments, or evidence remains **7:25**. Continuous customer work uses 35–56% of apparatus duty, but explicit experiment jobs occupy only 149–163 seconds in the reference campaigns; this game has no continuous quantum-training frontier. Most continuing activity is laboratory management and classical production. The specialist accepts these limitations as declared, while the provisional 90-minute first-human-play target and human enjoyment remain unverified. A human playtest is the next evidence needed for further pacing judgment.
